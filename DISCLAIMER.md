downloader can request pages and files from URLs you provide; those requests go
from your machine to the site you chose.

- You are responsible for the URLs you open in the browser, the URLs you submit

- Only download or read content you own or have permission to access.
- Follow the terms of service of every website you visit and the copyright laws

- Site recipes and extractor overlays only change how a page is parsed; they do

## Builds and site support

The public source tree ships a generic downloader. Local, private, or
third-party builds may load additional site recipes or an extractor overlay
through `YOPOMURA_DOWNLOADER_EXTRACTOR_MODULE` or
`YOPOMURA_DOWNLOADER_EXTRACTOR_PATH`. Those packs live outside the public
repository and are not endorsed by the project.
